About XHY

XHY Design Solutions provides professional design support for cabinet manufacturers, furniture brands, interior design firms, and showroom operators.

We help businesses expand their design capacity without the cost of building a larger in-house design team.

What We Do

Our services cover cabinetry design, interior visualization, CAD drafting, technical drawings, space planning, and showroom design.

  • Custom Cabinet Design
  • Interior Visualization & 3D Rendering
  • CAD Drafting & Technical Drawings
  • Space Planning & Interior Design
  • Showroom & Exhibition Design

Practical Design. Reliable Support.

Our strength lies in our understanding of cabinetry construction, manufacturing processes, and production requirements.

We don't simply create attractive visuals. We develop practical and buildable design solutions that work with real-world production requirements.
