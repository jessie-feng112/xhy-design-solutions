PROJECT OVERVIEW

Vintage Character, Thoughtfully Reimagined

This French vintage-inspired residence combines warm wood finishes, soft neutral tones, distinctive arched details, and carefully integrated cabinetry to create a welcoming home.

The open-plan living, dining, and kitchen area encourages shared everyday living, while tailored bedroom storage and dedicated private spaces support comfort and functionality.
