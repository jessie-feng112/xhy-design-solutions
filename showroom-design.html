PROJECT OVERVIEW

A Functional Showroom for Whole-Home Customization

This 132 m² showroom is designed to showcase whole-home customization solutions through a diverse range of interior spaces, cabinetry, materials, and display settings.

The design combines practical spatial planning with refined material presentation, creating an engaging environment where different cabinetry applications and interior styles can be clearly experienced and explored.
